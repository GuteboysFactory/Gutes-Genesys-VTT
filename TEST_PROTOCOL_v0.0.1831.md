# Test Protocol — v0.0.1831 Floating GM Dock Launcher

1. Update the QA system and confirm version `0.0.1831`.
2. Log in as GM and confirm the **GM Dock** bar appears near the lower-left canvas area.
3. Open Actor Directory and confirm the old GM Dock button is gone.
4. Click the floating bar without moving it and confirm GM Dock opens.
5. Drag the bar to a new position and confirm it does not open during the drag.
6. Reload Foundry and confirm the bar returns to its saved position.
7. Drag it toward every screen edge and confirm it remains fully inside the viewport.
8. Resize the browser window and confirm the bar is clamped back into view.
9. Log in as a non-GM user and confirm the bar is absent.
10. Regression: open GM Dock and verify Actor, XP and Encounter shortcuts still work.
